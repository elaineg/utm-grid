# Dana — Demand-gen marketer (Round 7, QR feature)

Tested cold on MacBook viewport. I tag 30+ links every week before Thursday; QR comes up
constantly for event signage, print one-pagers, and conference banners.

## Clarity — Yes
"Clean campaign links in a grid" + "tag a whole batch of 30+ campaign links at once" told me
exactly what it is in one scroll. The per-row "⊞ QR" button and the Tools → "QR Branding"
item were obvious. No login, nothing leaves your browser = the trust line I needed.

## Value — Yes (over my real workflow)
TODAY: I build UTMs in a Google Sheet formula, then for QR I paste each link into a free
QR site one at a time, and half of them slap a watermark or want an account for "branded"
ones. This collapses both jobs into one screen. The bulk "Download QR codes" gave me a real
ZIP (utm-qr-codes.zip) with a PER-LINK named PNG — `01-spring-sale-2026-newsletter-email.png`
— plus a contact-sheet.png. Named-by-campaign filenames is the detail that actually saves me
the rename grind. Empty rows were skipped automatically. That's a genuine 10-min-per-week win.

## The QR feature specifically — would I use it? YES, and yes it's a "this is free?!" moment
- Per-row QR popover with Download PNG / SVG: clean.
- Branding panel: PNG 512/1024/2048 + SVG, fg/bg color pickers, LIVE preview.
- Contrast guard WORKS: set fg #eeeeee on white → "Contrast 1.2:1", amber "Low contrast —
  pick a darker foreground… so scanners can read it" AND it disabled both Download PNG/SVG
  (verified disabled=true). That's the thing every free QR tool skips and it has burned me —
  a pretty unscannable code printed on 500 flyers. This catching it pre-print is the killer.
- Center logo: uploaded a PNG, it composited dead-center with a clear margin, note says it
  bumps error-correction to level H so it still scans, "composited locally — never uploaded."
  Branded + free + no account is exactly the combo I pay/sign-up for elsewhere. I'd screenshot
  this to my team channel today.

## Biggest friction
The whole QR feature is buried in the Tools ▼ dropdown ("QR Branding", "Download QR codes").
In one scroll I'd never know branded bulk QR exists — the per-row button gives a plain QR with
no hint that colors/logo/sizes/bulk-ZIP are a click away under Tools. The strongest selling
point is the least discoverable. (Note: clipboard copy not tested in headless; n/a.)

## One improvement
Surface QR at the top level: when a row is selected, show a "QR codes (bulk + branding)"
action near Export CSV, or add a one-line "Branded QR for every link" hint under the per-row
QR popover linking to the branding panel. Make the wow visible without spelunking Tools.

What holds it back from a 10: discoverability of the QR feature, nothing about the feature
itself — the execution is genuinely better than the paid tools I use.
